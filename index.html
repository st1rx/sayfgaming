Description

Formed in New Eden in November 2012. The corp is dormant; the people are not. Most of us are in the same Discord most nights, playing whatever the group is playing.

Three of us still undock, flying with Into the Ether under Out of the Blue. That is their own membership rather than a SAYF arrangement, and we don't speak for either.

There is no application and no interview. There is an invite link and a mod who will wave you through.
