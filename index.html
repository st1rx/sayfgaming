Who we are

A Discord with a thirteen‑year‑old inside joke attached

SAYF started as an EVE Online corporation in 2012. Corps come and go. This one didn't quite manage to die. The corp itself is dormant now, with nobody running ops under the ticker, but the people never left. Same Discord, same voice channel, most nights of the week.

A few of us still undock. Everyone else is playing something entirely different in the room next door. Both count.

  • Show up when you want

    No mandatory ops, no attendance spreadsheet. Most of us have jobs, kids, or both.

  • Bring a mic

    You don't have to talk. You do have to be able to.

  • Don't be a spy

    Old EVE habits die hard. So do old grudges. Neither is welcome here.

  • …but we're cool right?

    The name is a joke. The community isn't.

What we play

Home since 2012

EVE Online

There is a corp in New Eden called Sorry About Your Face, and it is dormant. Nobody has run ops under the ticker for a long time. What outlived it is this Discord, the jargon, and roughly half the jokes.

Three of us currently fly with Into the Ether, a PVP corp in the alliance Out of the Blue. That is their own membership and not a SAYF arrangement; the community has no formal tie to either. Curious? Say so in #eve and the three of them will tell you how it actually works.

Killboard 35,565 kills. 14.9 trillion ISK destroyed at 95% efficiency. The corp is dormant; the record is not going anywhere.
