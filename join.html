Join · SAYF Gaming

There's no application. There's a door.

SAYF isn't recruiting for a corp. We're a community, and joining means clicking a link, getting waved through the door and turning up. Here is exactly what you would be walking into.

Join the Discord
01

Is this you

You'll fit if

  • You would rather have a voice channel with someone in it than play alone.
  • You're coming back to EVE after a few years off and don't fancy starting cold.
  • You play four different games a month and want the same people in all of them.
  • You can take a joke aimed squarely at your face.

You won't if

  • You want a 300-pilot alliance with SRP, mandatory CTAs and an attendance spreadsheet.
  • You need a killboard to feel something.
  • You're here to recruit our people for somebody else.
  • Drama is your hobby. We aged out of that around 2015.
02

If you fly in New Eden

The corp is dormant. The pilots aren't.

Nobody has run ops under the SAYF ticker in a long time. The corp still exists in New Eden and it is dormant. What outlived it is this Discord.

Three of us currently fly with Into the Ether, a PVP corp in the alliance Out of the Blue. SAYF has no formal tie to either; it is three people's own membership. If that interests you, say so in #eve and they will point you at the right people. We bring the voice channel, not the doctrine, and we won't pretend to run a pipeline we don't run.

Killboard 35,565 kills. 14.9 trillion ISK destroyed at 95% efficiency. The corp is dormant; the record is not going anywhere.

Asked and answered

  • How do I actually get in?

    The invite drops you in #un-verified, which is the only channel you can see until you clear it. Verify with the bot to pick up the FACES role, or ask a mod in that channel and they'll do it by hand. Minutes, not days.

  • Do I have to play EVE?

    No. A good chunk of the server has never undocked and never will.

  • Is there a skill point requirement?

    None. Day-one alphas and 2003 characters get the same welcome.

  • Are there mandatory ops?

    No. We ping. You come, or you don't, and nobody keeps a tally.

  • Can I bring friends?

    Please do. Send them the same link you used.

  • Are you actually sorry about my face?

    No.
